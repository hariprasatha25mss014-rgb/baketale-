# Baketale Creative Brief & Reference Breakdown

## 1. Project Goal
Build a flagship, scroll-driven, brand-true web experience for **Baketale** ("A story in every bite") handcrafted brownies. The website translates artisanal handmade bakery charm into high-end interactive digital craftsmanship inspired by three reference benchmarks.

---

## 2. Reference Video Deconstruction

### Reference 1: Creamsy Ice Cream
- **Hero Mechanics**: Pinned 5-flavour viewport. Scrolling triggers a full background color transition, while the product image dynamically swaps with spring-driven scale, rotation, and soft blur transitions.
- **UI Architecture**: Masked headline slide-up, live size toggle pills with instant price updates, progress rail (01/05 counter, segmented scrub bars, jump dots), and dripping top border.
- **Section Transitions**: Organic wavy SVG dividers separating contrasting color sections.
- **Secondary Elements**: Tilted marquee ribbon, customer photo gallery with rounded cards, review cards with quotation marks, and an expandable FAQ accordion.

### Reference 2: The Deconstructed Gourmet Burger
- **Motion Principle**: Vertical layer separation ("Explosion of Flavor").
- **Scroll Behavior**: As the user scrolls into the pinned canvas section, individual layers (crinkle top crust, glossy chocolate ganache drizzle, gooey molten core, and dense cake base) float apart with realistic lighting, cast shadows, and suspended chocolate crumbs.
- **Typography & Tone**: Bold cinematic display headlines responding directly to scroll milestones.

### Reference 3: Maison Cacao Chocolaterie
- **Aesthetic**: Warm cream foundation, elegant serif headlines (*Fraunces*), high-contrast chocolate tones.
- **Scroll Melting Dynamics**: Chocolate artisan block softening, dripping, and melting into an organic chocolate pool as the user scrolls, leading into "Signature Flavors" collection cards.
- **Voice**: Understated luxury, sensory focus ("Crafted to Melt Your Senses").

---

## 3. Brand Foundations
- **Brand Name**: Baketale
- **Tagline**: "A story in every bite"
- **Specialty**: Handcrafted homemade brownies, bento cakes, custom gift boxes.
- **Reach**: Shipping across India.
- **Instagram**: `@baketale`
- **Voice & Tone**: Warm, playful, handmade, inviting, authentic small business warmth. No corporate jargon.

---

## 4. Anti-"AI Look" Constraints
1. **Curated Food Palette**: Strictly cocoa, cream, blush, and flavour-matched earth tones. No purple neon glows, generic gradients, or blue SaaS templates.
2. **One Motion Hero**: The Pinned Hero and Layer Anatomy are the centerpiece interactive moments. Other sections remain calm, polished, and readable.
3. **Typography Rigor**: Fraunces for display headings, DM Sans for clean high-legibility body copy, Caveat for intimate handwritten notes and stickers.
4. **Honest Content**: Exact menu items and rupees pricing. Sample testimonials are clearly marked. No fake stats or filler widgets.
